# CITE-016 — MABe22 (Sun et al., 2023)

## Citation

Sun JJ et al. *MABe22: A Multi-Species Multi-Task Benchmark for Learned Representations of Behavior.* Proceedings of the 40th International Conference on Machine Learning, PMLR 202:32936–32990 (2023).

## Relevance and extraction

MABe22 supplies video and pose/trajectory data across mice, beetles, ants and flies with downstream behavior and experimental-context tasks. It evaluates multiple learned representations and demonstrates that general human-action methods do not automatically transfer to animal behavior. It motivates explicit nuisance/context evaluation but contains no zebrafish and is not an unsupervised motif-validation replication of the present design.
