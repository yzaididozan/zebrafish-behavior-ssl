# CITE-015 — Azabou et al. (2023)

## Citation

Azabou M et al. *Relax, it doesn't matter how you get there: A new self-supervised approach for multi-timescale behavior analysis.* arXiv:2303.08811 (2023).

## Relevance and extraction

The work learns multi-timescale behavioral representations through future action-distribution prediction and evaluates them on MABe22 tasks. It is a direct animal-behavior SSL precedent, so the present project cannot claim self-supervised temporal behavior representation learning itself is new. It is not a zebrafish matched handcrafted-versus-SSL discovery study. Publication status must be refreshed before submission.
