# CITE-009 — Geng et al. (2023)

## Citation

Geng Y, Yates C, Peterson RT. *Social behavioral profiling by unsupervised deep learning reveals a stimulative effect of dopamine D3 agonists on zebrafish sociality.* Cell Reports Methods. 2023;3(1):100381. DOI: 10.1016/j.crmeth.2022.100381.

## Relevance and extraction

ZeChat uses unsupervised deep learning for high-resolution zebrafish social-behavior profiling and pharmacological screening. It is direct evidence that neither zebrafish unsupervised deep behavioral profiling nor automated discovery alone is novel. It does not match the present single-fish, handcrafted-versus-SSL comparison with the complete held-out-fish and nuisance-control battery.
