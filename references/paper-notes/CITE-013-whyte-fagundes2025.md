# CITE-013 — Whyte-Fagundes et al. (2025)

## Citation

Whyte-Fagundes P et al. *Automated detection of complex zebrafish seizure behavior at scale.* Communications Biology. 2025. DOI: 10.1038/s42003-025-08310-6.

## Relevance and extraction

The study combines 160-fps multi-camera recording, eight-point skeletal tracking, kinematic measurements and supervised machine learning across more than 2,700 larval zebrafish. It supports scalable pose-based behavioral quantification and tracking/kinematic validation. It is supervised and seizure-focused, so it does not match the present unlabeled representation-comparison claim.
