# SSL-002 — Xu & Wang (2024)

## Citation

Xu L, Wang S. *Technical Report: Masked Skeleton Sequence Modeling for Learning Larval Zebrafish Behavior Latent Embeddings.* arXiv:2403.15693. 2024.

## Publication Status

Technical report / preprint. Refresh status and version before manuscript submission.

## Why I Read It

Direct zebrafish self-supervised representation-learning precedent.

## Data / Input

Larval zebrafish skeletal sequences.

## Method

Sequence Spatial-Temporal Transformer (SSTFormer), combining transformer and CNN components.

## SSL Objective

Masked skeleton-sequence modeling.

## Representation

Latent behavioral embeddings encoding inter-frame and inter-joint structure.

## Main Result

Demonstrates direct feasibility of self-supervised latent behavioral representation learning in zebrafish.

## Limitations for Present Project

The targeted review did not identify the same matched design of:

- handcrafted baseline,
- temporal SSL,
- unsupervised discovery,
- held-out fish,
- identity/session/speed controls.

## What This Means for Zebrafish SSL

A claim of “first zebrafish SSL” is not defensible.
