# CITE-017 — Sun et al. (2024)

## Citation

Sun JJ et al. *Video Foundation Models for Animal Behavior Analysis.* bioRxiv. DOI: 10.1101/2024.07.30.605655.

## Relevance and extraction

The preprint evaluates frozen video foundation-model representations across animal-behavior classification, retrieval and localization tasks. It establishes general-purpose learned animal-video representations but does not match the primary zebrafish pose/trajectory SSL comparison or full nuisance-control framework. Publication and version status require refresh before submission.
