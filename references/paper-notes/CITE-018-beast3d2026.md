# CITE-018 — BEAST3D (Wang et al., 2026)

## Citation

Wang Y et al. *BEAST3D: Animal behavioral analysis and neural encoding from multi-view video via Gaussian splatting.* arXiv:2606.02937 (2026).

## Relevance and extraction

BEAST3D uses self-supervised held-out-view reconstruction to learn viewpoint-invariant 3D representations from calibrated multi-view animal video and evaluates transfer to pose estimation and neural encoding. It is a close follow-on animal SSL method but is not a zebrafish matched handcrafted-versus-SSL unsupervised discovery study. Its preprint/submission status requires refresh before manuscript submission.
