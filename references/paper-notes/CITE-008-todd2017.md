# CITE-008 — Todd et al. (2017)

## Citation

Todd JG, Kain JS, de Bivort BL. *Systematic exploration of unsupervised methods for mapping behavior.* Physical Biology. 2017;14(1):015002. DOI: 10.1088/1478-3975/14/1/015002.

## Relevance and extraction

The study compares seven unsupervised methods on wavelet-transformed fly leg-position data. It is retained as direct evidence that unsupervised mapping choices can change behavioral partitions and therefore should be governed and tested rather than selected from attractive plots. It is not zebrafish SSL and does not supply the present project's held-out-fish nuisance-control battery.
